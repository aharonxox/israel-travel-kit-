## Why travelers will find it useful

### Know what to say

Find supported common English-to-Hebrew phrases with gender-aware forms and pronunciation guidance. This is a curated phrase toolkit—not unlimited translation of any text.

### Pick your destination, then go

A simple form sends your destination to the navigation app suited to your journey: Google Maps for transit/walking and Waze for driving. Routes, schedules and travel times are supplied by those services, not calculated by Travel_ISL. An internet connection and third-party service may be required.

### Learn the details that matter

Readable tips help you think ahead about transport, parking, posted road restrictions, Shabbat/holiday schedule changes and checking current official advice. Every consequential guide links to its source and shows when it was reviewed.

### Stay in control of your subscription

Manage billing and cancellation from your account. No annual commitment: cancel future renewals and retain the access you already paid for.

## Navigation and safety microcopy

- **Transit:** Open transit directions in Google Maps
- **Walking:** Open walking directions in Google Maps
- **Driving:** Find this destination in Waze
- **Handoff notice:** You’re opening an external navigation service. Confirm the destination and check current conditions before traveling.

- **Buses:** Confirm route direction, destination, operating time and current operator notices before boarding. Do not use unsupported bus-number “safe/unsafe” lists.
- **Safety disclaimer:** Travel_ISL is a travel organizer, not an emergency service or real-time safety authority. Follow current official instructions and advisories.

## FAQ drafts

**Is this a one-time payment?**

No. Travel_ISL costs $8 USD per month and renews automatically until canceled.

**What happens if I cancel?**

Your next renewal is stopped and you retain access until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. Your account shows the exact date.

**Does Travel_ISL show live bus schedules?**

It opens your requested transit journey in Google Maps. Travel_ISL does not maintain its own live timetable or guarantee a service is operating. Check the operator’s current notices.

**Does everything work offline?**

Supported local phrase and curated content can be available offline where implemented. External maps, checkout, account recovery and refreshed advisories require connectivity. Do not claim full offline availability until tested.

**Will Waze prevent tickets or keep me out of every restricted area?**

No. It is a navigation aid. Follow traffic laws, posted restrictions and official instructions regardless of an app’s suggested route.
